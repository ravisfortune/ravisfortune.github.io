YouTube Integration & Data Usage

CricStream allows cricket scorers and club organizers to connect their YouTube account using secure Google OAuth to schedule broadcasts and stream live camera feeds directly to their channel.

We only request the permissions required to create and manage your live video broadcast (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube). Your streaming credentials remain securely on your device. We do not sell, share, or use your Google user data for advertising.
